How can your organization work with Penn State Engineering?

Ask in plain language. Every sentence in an answer is tied to an official College of Engineering page, and the sources are listed under it. If the official pages don't cover your question, the Navigator says so and points you to the right office instead of guessing.

Prototype. Answers are limited to the public pages that have been indexed, and are not a substitute for contacting the office directly.

How an answer is checked

1Your question is matched against indexed College of Engineering pages by both keywords and meaning.
2The five best passages go to a language model that may only use them, and must cite one after every sentence.
3Any sentence without a citation is removed before you see it.
4If the passages don't answer the question, you get a refusal and the closest official page.
